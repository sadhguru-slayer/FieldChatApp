Premium Chat UI/UX Redesign

Redesign the existing chat application's UI/UX only. Keep all functionality, logic, APIs, data, routes, and existing behavior exactly the same. Do not add, remove, or change features.

Design Direction

Use a premium minimal design system inspired by modern iOS/WhatsApp-quality messaging UX, with a Zinc-inspired neutral palette, subtle surface depth, refined typography, soft rounded corners, and restrained use of a single brand accent.

The UI should feel:

Minimal · Premium · Calm · Modern · Polished · Native-quality

Do not use heavy glassmorphism, excessive gradients, excessive borders, loud colors, or overly decorative components.

Focus on making the product feel premium through spacing, hierarchy, typography, surfaces, consistency, and subtle motion.

Add DM / Create Group

The current actions are too large and visually dominant.

Redesign them using best-practice messaging UX.

They should feel discoverable but not take over the interface. Use appropriate compact actions, menus, dialogs, or mobile bottom sheets depending on context.

The creation flows should feel simple, focused, and premium.

Search

Redesign the search experience and search modal to feel fast, clean, and integrated with the rest of the application.

Use clear hierarchy, proper spacing, polished results, and excellent mobile behavior.

Most Important

Do not redesign the functionality. Redesign how the functionality looks and feels.

First inspect the existing application and understand what is already available.

Then apply one consistent premium design language across every existing screen, so the app feels like one cohesive, intentionally designed product rather than a collection of different components.

The final result should feel like a high-end modern messaging app: minimal, elegant, highly usable, and especially excellent on mobile.
